title: "About"
---

Barbara Long is a textile artist whose practice is rooted in the textures of the natural world — the fragile, the fibrous, and the found. Working primarily with thread, fabric, and organic materials, her work explores the boundary between the handmade and the wild.

Her pieces often begin as drawings or walks, translating observation into slow, considered stitch. She is drawn to processes that resist speed: wrapping, binding, layering — acts that accumulate meaning over time.

Barbara has exhibited widely across the UK and internationally. She lives and works in Oxford.
